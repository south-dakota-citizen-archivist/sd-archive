Columns: geography (state or county), area, geoid (Census GEOID), year (and month), measure, value, report, source_url, page; the annual table adds year_to_date and through_month for a year still being reported.

Where this comes from

The South Dakota Department of Revenue publishes title and registration statistics: a report for each calendar year since 2018, with a page for the state and each county and a line for each month, and a historical report of each county's yearly totals since 2015. Each year here links to the report it comes from. Population is the Census Bureau's July 1 estimate for each year{% if population_note %} ({{ population_note }}){% endif %}.

How it's built

Each report is read from its PDF by its column headings, which are checked on every page, and the months on each page must add up to the page's total.

  • The state's totals are larger than its counties' added up for some types, most of all trucks and interstate fleet (IRP prorate) plates: the state counts registrations no county is credited with. Both are shown as published.
  • The historical report revises earlier years slightly; for the years it covers, its figures are used.
  • {{ partial.year if partial else "The current year" }} is shown only so far ({% if partial %}through {{ partial.month_name }}{% else %}as reported{% endif %}), and compared with the same months of the year before; the chart and map show full years.
  • Oglala Lakota County was Shannon County until 2015; every year here is after the change.
