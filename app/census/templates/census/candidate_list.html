Services directories the finders turned up that aren't sources yet: from ArcGIS Online items and web maps inside South Dakota, and from governments' own web domains. They're sorted by who publishes them: South Dakota governments first, then organizations and owners that name a South Dakota county, city, school district, tribe or state agency, then other governments, then the rest; within each, by how many South Dakota items point at them. Open one to scan its layers, see their fields and map a sample. Add opens the Add a GIS server form filled in; a rejected candidate stays rejected when a finder sees it again.
